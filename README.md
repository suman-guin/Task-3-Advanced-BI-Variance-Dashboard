# Task 3: Advanced BI & Variance Dashboard

An end-to-end Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project developed using the Superstore dataset. The project combines advanced SQL analysis, Excel-based variance modeling, interactive KPI visualization, and strategic business recommendations.

## Live Interactive Dashboard

[View the Executive Performance Dashboard](https://suman-guin.github.io/Task-3-Advanced-BI-Variance-Dashboard/)

## Project Objectives

* Apply advanced SQL techniques including CASE statements, subqueries, HAVING clauses, CTEs, and window functions.
* Calculate monthly variance and growth percentages.
* Build executive-level business KPIs.
* Analyze performance across regions, categories, segments, and customers.
* Identify profitability risks and growth opportunities.
* Develop an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard.
* Provide strategic, data-driven business recommendations.

## Key Business Insights

* The West region generated the highest sales and profit.
* Technology was the most profitable product category.
* Furniture recorded a low profit margin of approximately 2.49%.
* The Central region had the lowest regional profit margin at approximately 7.92%.
* Office Supplies achieved strong year-over-year growth in 2017.
* Orders with discounts of 30% or more produced an overall negative profit margin.
* December 2017 sales declined by approximately 29.23% compared with November 2017.
* The Consumer segment contributed the largest share of total revenue.
